The Story

Three chapters, three projects

2018 — 2021
Chapter One

Systems Thinking

I built a design system from twelve inconsistent screens into a shared language of over four hundred components, used by twenty engineers across four products. It taught me that good design scales only when it's written down.

This is where "UX designer" started to feel too small a title for what I was actually doing.

Founding Design Systems Lead

Orbit Design System

Unified four separate products under one component library and one voice, built for engineers to actually want to use.

98% component adoption across teams

2021 — 2023
Chapter Two

Leading Without a Title

I was mentoring two junior designers long before it was in my job description. When the title finally caught up with the work, I inherited a team of two and grew it to eight — while keeping a hand in real craft, not just process.

"The interface is the last ten percent. Understanding is the other ninety."
UX Lead

Northlight Patient Portal Redesign

Rebuilt the onboarding flow around real patient interviews instead of internal assumptions about what "simple" meant.
